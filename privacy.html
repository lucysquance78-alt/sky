Privacy policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Sky is a private personal assistant used only by Lucy Squance. This policy explains how Sky accesses, uses, stores and shares Google user data.

Who uses Sky

Only Lucy Squance, with her own Google account (lucysquance78@gmail.com). Sky has no other users and does not collect data about anyone who has not chosen to use it.

What Google data Sky accesses

With Lucy's permission, Sky can access her Gmail (read, draft, send and label), Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Sheets and Google Contacts (read only).

How the data is used

Google data is used only to carry out tasks Lucy asks for, such as summarising email, preparing her daily briefing, checking her diary or finding a document. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to helping Lucy.

Where the data is processed and stored

  • Sky runs on Lucy's own computer. Its sign-in and working memory are stored there.
  • To understand and respond to a request, the relevant content is sent to the AI model provider Sky uses (OpenAI, through Lucy's own ChatGPT subscription). It is processed only to answer that request.
  • Nothing is stored on any other server run for Sky.

Sharing

Google data is not sold, rented or shared with anyone, other than the AI model processing described above. It is never transferred to advertising platforms, data brokers or other information resellers.

Limited Use

Sky's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Keeping it secure

Sky's Google sign-in is kept on Lucy's computer, protected by her macOS user account. Access is limited to the permissions Lucy grants.

Removing access

Lucy can remove Sky's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or by deleting Sky's sign-in from her computer.

Contact

Questions about this policy: lucysquance78@gmail.com
